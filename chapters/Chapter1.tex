\chapter{Background}
\label{cap:chapter1}

This chapter outlines the problem posed by foodborne hazards, the critical
importance of early detection, and current technologies on how to detect them.
Section \ref{sec:foodborne_hazards} lists the most common hazards found in food
and provides statistical data on annual cases, highlighting the magnitude of the
challenge for public health. This chapter includes a more in depth introduction
for ammonium and histamine, the two target analytes to be detected in this
project. Section \ref{sec:traditional_detection} describes the traditional
methods used to detect foodborne hazards, highlighting their advantages and
limitations, establishing the rationale for developing the sensors and
biosensors described in Section \ref{sec:sensors}. After outlining the
fundamental characteristics of sensors, the chapter provides a more detailed
analysis of Field Effect Transistors (FETs) (Section \ref{sec:FET}) and more
specifically, Electrolyte-Gated FETs (Section \ref{sec:EGFET}), \ie{} the
platform herein developed. Finally, Section \ref{sec:simulations} introduces
mathematical modeling and simulations of EG-FETs, with the aim to better
describe the platform behaviour.
