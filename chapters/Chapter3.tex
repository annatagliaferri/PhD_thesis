\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:stability_conclusion}

\begin{color}{red}

Membrane changes EDL capacitance and ionic accessibility → explains current reduction and opposite Vth/on–off trends.

Slow ion migration / membrane charge relaxation causes hysteresis that decays with repeated sweeps (~60 min).

Fabrication non-uniformity in the small devices (spray + mask) causes increased variability and prevents consistent stabilization; local defects change effective C\_EDL and contact/percolation.

Contact resistance and percolation density differences in the scaled devices increase sensitivity to the above effects.

Minor Faradaic surface chemistry may still occur at very small levels and alter doping slowly, but is not dominant (gate leakage small).
\end{color}
