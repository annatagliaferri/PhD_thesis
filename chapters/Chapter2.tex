\chapter{Methodology}
\label{cap:chapter2}

The Materials and Methods chapter provides a comprehensive list and description of the materials and procedures employed to reach the goal of this project, \ie{} the successful fabrication and characterization of a sensor based on an Electrolyte-Gated Field-Effect Transistor for the detection of foodborne hazards. This chapter describes the instrumentation, experimental designs and  protocols, to ensure reliability and reproducibility of the results. Section \ref{sec:materials} lists all the materials and instruments used; Section \ref{sec:fabrication} and \ref{sec:cntDeposition} describe the protocols used for device fabrication; Section \ref{sec:membrane_stabilization} reports the improvements made on the EG-FET transducer with respect to state-of-the-art devices; Section \ref{sec:functionalization} outlines the strategies employed to functionalize the gate of the EG-FET and thus obtain a sensor; finally, Section \ref{sec:characterization} provides a description of the techniques used to fully characterize the device, in order to obtain as much information as possible.
